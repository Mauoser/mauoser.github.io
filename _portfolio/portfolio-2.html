Roomalize is an AI-powered architectural visualization SaaS that transforms 2D floor plans into photorealistic 3D renders using React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Puter.js, and Gemini AI. Puter.js powers a serverless infrastructure with KV storage and cloud hosting; Gemini AI drives photorealistic rendering.
